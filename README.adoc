## Virtual Research Environments
A virtual research environment (VRE) or virtual laboratory is an online system helping researchers collaborate.

## Scope

### Vision
The vision of this framework is to provide a structured approach to assess and enhance the readiness of virtual research labs, ensuring they meet the needs of researchers and stakeholders effectively.

### Context
The context of this framework is rooted in the increasing complexity of research environments and the need for effective collaboration tools. As research becomes more interdisciplinary and data-driven, virtual research labs must evolve to support diverse workflows and stakeholder requirements. Additionally, the increasing number of virtual research labs necessitates a standardized approach to assess their readiness and effectiveness.

### Goals
The goals of this framework include:

1. Establishing clear criteria for assessing the readiness of virtual research labs.
2. Providing a roadmap for enhancing the capabilities of these labs.
3. Facilitating collaboration and knowledge sharing among researchers.
4. Supporting the development of best practices for virtual research environments.
